COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING AND MANAGEMENT PUNNAPRA

The college of engineering and management punnapra is an engineering college in alappuzha district of kerala,established in 2008 under the co-operative academy of proffesional education.The college is affiliated to kerala university (till 2015)and APJ Abdul Kalam Technical University(KTU)from 2015 and is approved by all india council for technical education(AICTE).
